## Acceptance

The model is accepted if, for every measured output, its RMSE on each fitting
experiment and on the validation experiment is at most √2 ≈ 1.414 times the
noise standard deviation stated above for that output.

Before you deliver, compare every RMSE with this threshold. If any lies
above it, repair the model, numerically or structurally, and compare again.
If you deliver a model that still lies above the threshold, say so in the
report and explain why.
